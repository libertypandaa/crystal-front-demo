# Google Play listing draft — en-US

App name: Crystal Front

Short description:
Offline crystal duels: match gems, shift the front, challenge the AI.

Full description:
Crystal Front is an offline tactical puzzle duel on an 8×8 board. Swap adjacent crystals to make lines of three or more. Clear rival territory, push the front, and plan around chain reactions as the board refills.

Choose your duel:
• Score Target: be first to reach the selected score.
• Last Move: play until no scoring moves remain.
• AI Duel: watch two computer players face off, with pause and speed controls.

Tune AI difficulty and the target score. Use Bomb, Line, Mix, and Color bonuses when a move calls for a bigger strike. Earn Lighthouse Rays through matches and spend them on bonuses in the in-game shop. Rays and bonuses are local gameplay resources; there are no real-money purchases.

Your profile and match are saved on this device so you can pause and continue later. Gameplay works offline. Optional Firebase Analytics is off by default and can be changed in Settings.

Crystal Front is a single-player game against AI, with an AI-versus-AI observer mode. It has no multiplayer, ads, or account-based progress sync.
